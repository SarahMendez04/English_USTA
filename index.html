01 · The Problem

The Problem We Are Addressing

Social media is part of our daily life. People use it to communicate, study, have fun, and share information. However, excessive use of social media can affect personal relationships. People may spend less time with their family and friends and have less face-to-face communication.

Sometimes, the people next to us deserve more attention than the screen in our hands.

02 · How to Help

How We Are Helping

Our action plan is simple: create moments where people can put their phones away, have fun together, and reconnect in real life.

1

“Phone-Free Hours” Campaign

We promote specific times of the day, such as meals or family time, when everyone commits to putting their phones away.

2

Organize Face-to-Face Activities

We host regular events like board game nights, outdoor walks, or community dinners where the main goal is to interact in person.

3

Educational Workshops

We give short talks and share informative materials about the effects of excessive social media use and the benefits of real-life connections.

03 · Get Involved

Get Involved!

We will invite friends, family, and classmates to join our activities. We will also use social media itself to create a group where we post about upcoming offline events and share success stories from people who have improved their relationships by disconnecting.

04 · Motivation

Our Motivation and Expected Impact

Why does this matter?

We want to offer a sense of community and fun. People are more motivated to join when they see that they are not alone in feeling overwhelmed by social media and that our activities are a fun way to relax and reconnect with others.

Through these actions, we aim to:
✓

Strengthen Personal Relationships

Encourage more quality time and meaningful conversations among family and friends.

✓

Raise Awareness

Educate the community about the negative effects of excessive social media use on mental health and relationships.

✓

Create Healthy Habits

Inspire long-term change in how people use their phones, promoting a healthier balance between the digital world and real life.

✓

Build a Supportive Community

Create a group of people who support each other in their goal to disconnect and connect more with those around them.
